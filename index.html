Frequently Asked Questions

What is SaleStation?
SaleStation is a cloud-ready Point of Sale (POS) and business management platform built for businesses in South Africa, Botswana, Lesotho, and Namibia. It lets you manage sales, inventory, cashiers, receipts, and subscriptions - all from a single interface.
What plans are available and how much do they cost?
SaleStation offers three plans, all billed in South African Rand (ZAR):

Trial - Free for 7 days. 1 cashier, up to 15 items. No payment required.
Starter - R200/month. 2 cashiers, up to 50 items, weekly statement exports, 2-day grace period.
Premium - R500/month. Unlimited cashiers and items, multi-store (up to 3 locations), audit logs, weekly/monthly/yearly exports, 2-day grace period.

International customers (Botswana, Lesotho, Namibia) are charged in ZAR; your bank converts the amount at the prevailing exchange rate.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new business gets a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. The trial gives you access to core POS features with a limit of 1 cashier and 15 items. After 7 days, you can upgrade to a paid plan to continue using SaleStation.
How does payment work?
Payments are processed securely through Paystack, a leading African payment platform. You can pay with any major debit or credit card. All subscription charges are in ZAR. You can renew or upgrade your plan at any time from the Subscription page inside your dashboard.
What happens when my subscription expires?
Paid plans (Starter and Premium) include a 2-day grace period after expiry, during which you can still log in and renew without losing access. After the grace period, your account is suspended until you renew. The Renew button becomes available in the final 2 days of your billing cycle. Trial plans have no grace period.
Can I add multiple cashiers?
Yes. The Starter plan supports up to 2 cashiers, and the Premium plan supports unlimited cashiers. Each cashier gets their own login and can process sales from the POS screen. As the admin, you can add, edit, suspend, or remove cashiers from the Cashiers section of your dashboard.
Does SaleStation support multiple store locations?
Multi-store support is available on the Premium plan, allowing up to 3 store locations under a single business account. Each branch has its own isolated inventory — cashiers only see and sell items assigned to their location. You can also mark items as shared to make them available across all branches. Revenue and transaction reports are tracked per branch from the moment a cashier is assigned, keeping your financial data accurate across locations. Manage everything from the Multi-Store section of your admin dashboard.
How does branch inventory work?
Each branch on the Premium plan has its own independent inventory. When you add or edit an item, you can assign it to a specific branch — or leave it unassigned to make it a shared item visible at all locations. Cashiers assigned to a branch only see their branch's items plus any shared items, never items belonging to another branch. This means your staff can't accidentally ring up stock from a different location.
What currencies are supported?
SaleStation supports four local currencies for displaying prices and receipts: ZAR (South Africa), BWP (Botswana), LSL (Lesotho), and NAD (Namibia). All subscription billing, however, is processed in ZAR regardless of your country.
Can I export my sales data?
Yes. Starter plan users can export weekly sales statements. Premium plan users can export weekly, monthly, and yearly statements, plus audit action logs - all as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV files directly from the dashboard.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. You can cancel at any time from the Subscription page in your admin dashboard. After cancelling, you retain full access until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are issued for unused days, but you won't be charged again.
How do I contact support?
You can reach us via the Contact section on this page, or through the Contact Support option inside your dashboard. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on business days.
